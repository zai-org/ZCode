# Motion

Motion makes it clear what changed and where it went, and makes waiting bearable; motion unrelated to what the user is doing is decoration and stays out. It usually belongs to a state change in place (toggle, saved, validation), something entering or leaving (menu, dialog, drawer, toast, expanding row, a page or slide), continuity between views, waits (loading, sending, empty or success states) and direct manipulation (drag, swipe, resize); where one of these is instant, have a reason.

It stays off what the user is reading or comparing and is never the only carrier of a change. Durations and curves: `design-resources/craft/animation-discipline.md`.
